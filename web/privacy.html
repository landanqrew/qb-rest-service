QuickBooks Online Integration

1Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how this integration with QuickBooks Online (the "Integration") handles data. In this policy, "Operator" means the company that has deployed and operates the Integration. The Integration is an internal, single-tenant tool used only by the Operator to connect its own applications to its own QuickBooks Online company. It is not a consumer-facing product and does not collect data from members of the public.

2Data we access

When authorized by an administrator of the Operator, the Integration accesses the accounting records the Operator authorizes from its QuickBooks Online company, which may include:

  • Customer records (names, contact and billing details).
  • Products and services (items) used on invoices.
  • Invoices and their line items.

This data is read from, and written to, QuickBooks Online on behalf of the Operator in the course of its normal business operations.

3How we use it

Data is used solely to synchronize the Operator's own applications with QuickBooks Online — for example, looking up a customer, selecting an item, and creating or updating the corresponding invoice. The Integration performs no analytics, profiling, advertising, or sale of data.

4Data storage and retention

The Integration holds no business data of its own. It does not maintain a database or cache of QuickBooks records; every request is made live against QuickBooks Online. The only credential the Integration stores is the OAuth token needed to call QuickBooks on the Operator's behalf, kept in a secret store and used only to maintain the authorized connection.

5Data sharing

QuickBooks data is not shared with any third party. It moves only between the Operator's own systems and Intuit's QuickBooks Online service.

6Security

The Integration communicates with QuickBooks Online over HTTPS and restricts access to the Operator's authorized systems. OAuth credentials are kept in a managed secret store.

7Intuit / QuickBooks Online

Data within QuickBooks Online is also subject to Intuit's own privacy policy and terms. This policy does not modify or supersede any agreement between the Operator and Intuit. "QuickBooks" and "Intuit" are trademarks of Intuit Inc.

8Changes

This policy may be updated as the Integration evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

9Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy should be directed to the administrator responsible for the Integration at the operating company.
