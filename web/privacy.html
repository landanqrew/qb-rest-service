Privacy Policy

MWL Lab Intake — QuickBooks Online Integration

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Martin Water Labs ("MWL") Lab Intake integration with QuickBooks Online (the "Integration") handles data. The Integration is an internal, single-tenant tool used only by Martin Water Labs to connect its Lab Intake application to its own QuickBooks Online company. It is not a consumer-facing product and does not collect data from members of the public.

2. Data we access

When authorized by an MWL administrator, the Integration accesses the following from the Martin Water Labs QuickBooks Online company:

  • Customer records (names, contact and billing details).
  • Products and services (items) used on invoices.
  • Invoices and their line items.

This data is read from, and written to, QuickBooks Online on behalf of Martin Water Labs in the course of MWL's normal accounting operations.

3. How we use it

Data is used solely to synchronize lab orders between MWL's Lab Intake application and QuickBooks Online — for example, looking up a customer, selecting an item, and creating or updating the corresponding invoice. The Integration performs no analytics, profiling, advertising, or sale of data.

4. Data storage and retention

The Integration holds no business data of its own. It does not maintain a database or cache of QuickBooks records; every request is made live against QuickBooks Online. The only credential the Integration stores is the OAuth token needed to call QuickBooks on MWL's behalf, kept in Google Cloud Secret Manager and used only to maintain the authorized connection.

5. Data sharing

QuickBooks data is not shared with any third party. It moves only between Martin Water Labs' own systems (the Lab Intake application and the Integration) and Intuit's QuickBooks Online service.

6. Security

The Integration runs on Google Cloud Run, communicates with QuickBooks Online over HTTPS, and restricts access to authorized Martin Water Labs systems. OAuth credentials are stored in Google Cloud Secret Manager.

7. Intuit / QuickBooks Online

Data within QuickBooks Online is also subject to Intuit's own privacy policy and terms. This policy does not modify or supersede any agreement between Martin Water Labs and Intuit. "QuickBooks" and "Intuit" are trademarks of Intuit Inc.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated as the Integration evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy should be directed to the Martin Water Labs administrator responsible for the Integration.
