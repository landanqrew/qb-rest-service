QuickBooks Integration

This service connects Martin Water Labs’ internal Lab Intake system to QuickBooks Online. It keeps customer records, products and services, and invoices aligned with our accounting system so laboratory work is billed accurately and promptly.

What it does

  • Looks up customers and product/service items in QuickBooks Online.
  • Creates and updates invoices for completed laboratory work.

Who can use it

This is an internal tool operated by Martin Water Labs against its own QuickBooks Online company. It is not offered to the public and has no public sign-up.
