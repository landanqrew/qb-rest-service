This policy describes how the Martin Water Labs QuickBooks Integration (the “Service”) and this website handle data. The Service is an internal tool operated by Martin Water Labs (“MWL”) for its own business; it is not offered to the public.

What data the Service accesses

The Service accesses MWL’s own QuickBooks Online company via Intuit’s APIs, limited to the data needed for laboratory billing:

  • customer records (names, billing details);
  • product and service items;
  • invoices and their line items.

How it is used

Exclusively to support MWL’s internal lab-intake and billing workflows. Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

What is stored

The Service stores no QuickBooks business data. Every request is served live from QuickBooks Online, which remains the system of record. The only data the Service persists are the OAuth credentials Intuit issues to it, held in Google Cloud Secret Manager with access restricted to the Service’s own runtime identity.

Security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • The Service rejects unauthenticated requests; access is limited to MWL’s internal systems and named administrators.
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest by Google Cloud Secret Manager.

This website

These pages are static. They set no cookies and use no analytics, trackers, or third-party scripts.

Changes

MWL may update this policy from time to time. The version published at this URL is the current one.
