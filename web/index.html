QuickBooks Online Integration

A private, single-tenant integration that syncs an operator's own business application with its QuickBooks Online company.

What this is

This site supports a private integration between an internal business application and QuickBooks Online. The integration lets the operator's own tools read and write accounting records in the operator's QuickBooks Online company, so day-to-day operations flow into accounting without manual re-entry.

Who it's for

A single-tenant, internal business integration operated by the company that deployed it for its own use. It is not a public product, it is not offered to other companies, and there is no end-user sign-up here.

Data handling

The integration stores no QuickBooks business data of its own — every request is made live against QuickBooks Online. Only the OAuth token needed to maintain the connection is kept, in a managed secret store.

How it uses QuickBooks

  • Reads accounting records such as customers, products/services (items), and invoices.
  • Creates and updates invoices and their line items.
  • Updates or deactivates records as the operator's business requires.

Access is authorized by an administrator of the operating company through Intuit's standard OAuth consent flow.
