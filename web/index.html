MWL Lab Intake — QuickBooks Online Integration

A private integration operated by Martin Water Labs.

What this is

This site supports the integration between the Martin Water Labs (MWL) Lab Intake web application and QuickBooks Online. The integration lets MWL's internal Lab Intake tool read customers, items, and invoices from, and write invoices to, the Martin Water Labs QuickBooks Online company so that lab orders flow into accounting without manual re-entry.

Who it's for

This is a single-tenant, internal business integration for Martin Water Labs only. It is not a public product and is not offered to other companies. There is no end-user sign-up here.

How it uses QuickBooks

  • Reads customers, products/services (items), and invoices.
  • Creates and updates invoices and their line items.
  • Deactivates items when the lab retires a test code.

Access is authorized by a Martin Water Labs administrator through Intuit's standard OAuth consent flow. The integration stores no QuickBooks business data of its own — every request is made live against QuickBooks Online.
